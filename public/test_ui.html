🚀 ECO A+ PRO — Content UI & Roles Demo
Client Approval/Update Flow • Admin/Worker Diff Viewer • D4U Writer Auto-Fill & Draft
👤 Client View (Product Status: Generated):
Jab Writer content ready kar deta hai, to Client ke samne ye 6 boxes aate hain. Client har box ko review karta hai:
• Agar content pasand aye: APPROVED click karta hai (content lock ho kar Worker ke pass chala jata hai).
• Agar client kisi box mein koi change kare: UPDATED button foran green ho kar enable ho jata hai aur APPROVED disable ho jata hai. Sath hi neechay real-time Added / Deleted Diff show hota hai!
🔍 Admin & Worker View (Product Status: Updated):
Client ne content mein tabdeeli ki aur UPDATED click kiya. Ab Admin aur Worker ko card mein:
1. Har wo box jis mein client ne changes kiye hain, us ke upar ✏ Modified ka badge aur amber border nazar aata hai (Image 2 aur Image 4 dekhein!).
2. Card ke neechay 🔍 Client Changes — ■ Added   ■ Deleted ka complete visual word-by-word diff bar nazar aata hai, jahan clear pata chalta hai ke client ne kya delete kiya aur kya add kiya!
✍ D4U Writer View (Product Status: Pending):
Writer ke pass 2 asaan tareeqe hain content create karne ke:
Tareeqa A (Direct): Writer direct har image ke box (Image 1, Image 2, etc.) mein content type kar sakta hai.
Tareeqa B (1-Click Auto-Fill): Writer upar 📋 Auto-Fill All Boxes from Single Text button par click karke ChatGPT / Gemini / Doc ka poora text aik hi baar paste karega, aur system automatically 6 boxes mein distribute kar dega!
Phir writer neechay 💾 Save Draft dabata hai aur product direct Client Approval ke liye chali jati hai!
🔄 Info + A Plus Phase Transition:
Dono phases ka taaluq dekhein:
• Phase 1: Infographics active hoti hai aur team us par kaam karti hai.
• Phase 2: Jab A+ Banners shuru hota hai, to Infographics ka 6-image template 🔒 Lock (Read-only Reference) ban jata hai taa ke worker/client purana content refer kar sakein, aur neechay A+ Banners (Phase 2) ka 4-Banner Grid activate ho jata hai!
